🕵️ The Mole's Tale

Build a story one word at a time — while smuggling in your secret word undetected.

How it plays: 3–8 players build a story together, one word each per turn. Everyone gets a secret word to sneak into the story. Afterwards, everyone votes on who smuggled which word. Points for sneaking yours in unseen — and for catching the other moles. 🐾
